Is Physics Universe really free?

Yes.
Physics Universe is always free for all interested in physics.

Do I get a certificate upon completion?

Yes!
Once you complete all the modules and pass the final assessment for a course, you will receive a verifiable digital certificate of completion. You can easily share this on LinkedIn, add it to your CV, or print it for your records.

How does Physics Universe support students at different levels?

Physics Universe helps students start where they are and move forward at their own pace.

Students who need extra support can build foundational skills step by step, while students who are ready can continue advancing, so every l earner makes progress.

Yes. Research shows that students who practice more skills on Physics Universe see greater academic gains.
